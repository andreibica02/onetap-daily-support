OneTap Daily Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

OneTap Daily is designed with privacy in mind. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with the developer or third parties.

Data Collection

OneTap Daily does not use:

  • analytics;
  • advertising;
  • tracking technologies;
  • user accounts;
  • cloud databases;
  • remote servers for storing habit data;
  • third-party data collection services.

Data Stored on Your Device

Information created in OneTap Daily is stored locally on your device. This may include:

  • habit names;
  • habit start dates;
  • daily Yes / No check-ins;
  • habit history;
  • reminder preferences;
  • app settings.

This information is not transmitted to the developer.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, OneTap Daily uses local notifications on your device. Habit names may appear in notification content and may be visible on your Lock Screen depending on your iOS notification settings.

Device Backups

Because OneTap Daily stores information locally, app data may be included in iCloud backups or computer backups depending on your device and backup settings.

Permissions

OneTap Daily may request permission to send notifications.

The app does not request access to:

  • location;
  • contacts;
  • photos;
  • camera;
  • microphone;
  • Health data.

Third-Party Services

OneTap Daily does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

OneTap Daily does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's functionality or privacy practices change. The latest version will always be published on this page.
