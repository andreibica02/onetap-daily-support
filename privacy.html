OneTap Daily Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

OneTap Daily is designed with privacy in mind. This policy explains what the app collects and how it is used.

Data You Create

Your habits, check-ins, history, gardens and settings are used only to provide the app's features. They are stored on your device and may be included in your iCloud or computer backups.

Anonymous Usage Data

To improve the app and fix problems, OneTap Daily sends anonymous usage data to PostHog, an analytics service in the European Union. This includes actions in the app, summary numbers such as how many habits you have, device and app version information, and crash reports. Habit names and anything you type are never sent, and the data is not linked to your name, email address or Apple ID.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings, under Privacy.

Services

The app downloads its settings from PostHog and asks the developer's server, hosted on Cloudflare, for the current time and the minimum supported app version. These requests do not include your habit data.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, habit names may appear in notifications and on your Lock Screen, depending on your iOS settings. The app does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or Health data.

Your Choices

Deleting the app removes the data stored on your device. For any other request about your data, contact us at the address below.

Changes to This Policy

This policy will be updated when the app's features or privacy practices change. The latest version is always published on this page.
